# Name Ideas — Pāṭala Rebrand

*Alien/space-themed, relevant to the product*

---

## The product does one thing

> Tracks which conclusions still follow from changing evidence.

It's a **continuity layer** for agent knowledge. A **freshness oracle**. A **provenance guardian**.

---

## Name candidates

### 1. **Continuum**
> "When evidence changes, Continuum tells you what still follows."

- Clean, memorable
- Implies continuity, ongoing, persistent
- Works as: "Continuum for OpenAIRE", "Continuum agent memory"
- Domain: continuum.dev / continuum.ai
- Risk: generic (other products use this name)

### 2. **Drift**
> "Drift detects when your conclusions have drifted from the evidence."

- Simple, evocative
- Implies movement away from truth
- Works as: "Drift: agent knowledge freshness"
- Short, punchy
- Risk: some other products use this

### 3. **Epoch**
> "Epoch marks when evidence changes and what it affects."

- Science/physics connotation (epoch = point in time)
- Implies measurement, precision
- Works as: "Epoch: evidence epoch tracking"
- Distinctive

### 4. **Ephemeral**
> "Pāṭala was about permanence. Ephemeral tracks what doesn't last."

- Paradoxical, memorable
- Implies transience of knowledge
- Works as: "Ephemeral: what stays justified?"
- Risk: sounds like a password manager

### 5. **Ground Truth**
> "Ground Truth verifies what conclusions remain grounded in evidence."

- Technical, precise
- Implies verification, grounding
- Works as: "Ground Truth for agent knowledge"
- Risk: generic, other ML products use this

### 6. **Sentinel**
> "Sentinel guards the validity of agent conclusions over time."

- Implies protection, vigilance
- Works as: "Sentinel: agent knowledge guardian"
- Distinctive, professional

### 7. **Epoch Shift**
> "Epoch Shift detects when evidence shifts and what it breaks."

- Combines time + change
- Implies detection + consequence
- Works as: "Epoch Shift: evidence change detection"
- Distinctive, memorable

### 8. **Anchor**
> "Anchor keeps agent conclusions anchored to their evidence."

- Implies stability, grounding
- Works as: "Anchor: evidence-grounded agent memory"
- Clean, professional

---

## My recommendation

**Best for hackathon:** **Drift** — short, evocative, easy to explain. "Drift detects when your conclusions have drifted from the evidence."

**Best for product:** **Continuum** — implies continuity, persistence, ongoing verification. "Continuum for OpenAIRE."

**Most distinctive:** **Epoch Shift** — combines time + change, hard to confuse with anything else.

**If keeping Pāṭala:** Keep it. The name has history and meaning (Sanskrit for "layer/level"). The hackathon judges won't care about the name as much as the demo.
